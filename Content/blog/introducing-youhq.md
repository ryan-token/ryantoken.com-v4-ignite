Today I'm releasing a new app I've been building in my spare time:

## YouHQ: Life Organizer

YouHQ is a reference guide for life's important details. It's a one-stop shop to keep track of info about your home, vehicles, finances, media, and career.

It's available for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. It's built natively with [Swift](https://www.swift.org) and [SwiftUI](https://developer.apple.com/xcode/swiftui/), and the whole thing ships to all four platforms from one codebase with a few platform-specific tweaks.

I have a full-time job building [Daysheets](https://www.daysheets.com), so this was a nights-and-weekends project. But it's an app I've wanted for myself for a long time.

## Why I built it

I needed a reference guide for my life.

What color is the paint in the living room? When should I get my next oil change? Which account is that old HSA sitting in? What was the model name of that TV I bought three years ago? Where did I write down the serial number for the water heater?

These are the small, boring details of being an adult, and they live in a dozen scattered places. A note here, a screenshot there, a drawer full of paper somewhere else. I could never find any of it when I actually needed it.

So I built the thing I wished existed: a single, private place to keep all of it. I made it for myself, used it for a while, and eventually figured it might be useful to other people too. So here it is!

## What's inside

YouHQ organizes your life into five areas: home, vehicles, money, media, and career.

**Home.** Track multiple residences with their utilities, insurance, and paint colors. Schedule maintenance reminders, attach photos, and see the total monthly cost of ownership for each property.

## Your data is yours

This is personal information, so security and privacy is a hard requirement.

There are no YouHQ servers. Everything stays on your devices and syncs directly through iCloud using your Apple Account. Sensitive fields are encrypted on-device with [AES-256-GCM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois/Counter_Mode) encryption. And if you want to, you can share your data with others securely over iCloud.

The source code for YouHQ is fully open source and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/ryan-token/YouHQ).
